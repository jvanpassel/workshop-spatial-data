The structure of the workshop was:

Day 1: theory on spatial data and climate data in the morning, hands on with climate data in Google Earth Engine (GEE) and R in the afternoon

Day 2: theory on optical satellite data in the morning, hands on with satellite data in GEE and R in the afternoon

Day 3: Prepare personal project using satellite or climate data on region of choice in the morning, short presentations in the afternoon

On the last slide of the two 'practical' presentations, you can find links to full working GEE scripts, with one focusing on the analyses with precipitation data and the other one (called 'example France') with temperature data.

The included R scripts do the analyses using precipitation data, while example_france is a full working example with all necessary data for temperature data.
